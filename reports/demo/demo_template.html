Loading the model: 49 minutes to 1

The trainer faulted the 59 GB model in over network storage at ~25 MB/s. Copying one 5 GB file at a time into memory with one sequential read, then loading from that copy, made it ~1 minute.

Base model vs fine-tuned, on companies the model never saw

152 risk-factor passages from MS, GOOGL, PFE and WMT 10-K filings (held out from training). For each, the model must name the most material risk class, its severity, and quote the passage word for word.

All numbers are measured on the CIQ fb-hack cluster on 14–15 Nov 2026 (Fuzzball workflows; per-step logs and summary.json of each run). Fine-tuned = LoRA adapter from step 91 of a 140-step data-parallel run (best validation loss); answers generated greedily, max 300 tokens; scores match scripts/08_score.py. Correct labels come from the teacher model (Nemotron 3 Ultra) used to build the dataset; 60 of them are queued for human review in data/sft/gold_review.csv and none is reviewed yet.
